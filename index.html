Modus Ponens

Metode Afirmasi

Premis 1: p → q
Premis 2: p
Kesimpulan: ∴ q

Modus Tollens

Metode Penyangkalan

Premis 1: p → q
Premis 2: ~q
Kesimpulan: ∴ ~p

Silogisme

Rantai Sebab-Akibat

Premis 1: p → q
Premis 2: q → r
Kesimpulan: ∴ p → r

📖 Materi: Logika Deduktif & If Clause

1. Modus Ponens

  • If he studies hard, he will pass the exam. He studies hard.
    Result: He will pass the exam.
  • If it rains, the grass gets wet. It is raining.
    Result: The grass gets wet.
  • If she has a ticket, she enters. She has a ticket.
    Result: She enters.
  • If they win, they are champions. They win.
    Result: They are champions.
  • If the engine is hot, the fan starts. The engine is hot.
    Result: The fan starts.

2. Modus Tollens

  • If the cake is done, it smells good. It doesn't smell good.
    Result: The cake is not done.
  • If he is a bird, he has wings. He does not have wings.
    Result: He is not a bird.
  • If the battery is full, it turns on. It does not turn on.
    Result: The battery is not full.
  • If she is home, her car is here. Her car is not here.
    Result: She is not home.
  • If water boils, it's 100°C. It's not 100°C.
    Result: The water does not boil.

3. Silogisme

If you eat too much, you get a stomachache. If you get a stomachache, you need medicine.
Result: If you eat too much, you need medicine.
If it's a holiday, the office is closed. If the office is closed, we stay home.
Result: If it's a holiday, we stay home.
If he gets promoted, his salary increases. If his salary increases, he buys a car.
Result: If he gets promoted, he buys a car.
If the wind is strong, waves are high. If waves are high, fishing is dangerous.
Result: If the wind is strong, fishing is dangerous.
If you practice, you master it. If you master it, you win.
Result: If you practice, you win.

4. If Clause (Conditional Sentences)

Type 1: Future Possible

(If + Present, Will + V1)

  • If it rains, I will stay home.
  • If you invite her, she will come.
  • If they work hard, they will finish.
  • If he calls, I will answer.
  • If we hurry, we will catch it.

Type 2: Present Unreal

(If + Past, Would + V1)

  • If I had money, I would buy it.
  • If she knew, she would tell him.
  • If I were you, I wouldn't do it.
  • If they studied, they would pass.
  • If he spoke English, he would get it.

Type 3: Past Unreal

(If + Past Perfect, Would have + V3)

  • If I had woken up, I would have caught it.
  • If she had studied, she would have passed.
  • If it had rained, it would have been wet.
  • If invited, I would have gone.
  • If he had looked, he wouldn't have crashed.
